## Welcome

Anna: Welcome to a short demo of two voices. I'm Anna, and this is Ben.

Ben: Hello. Today we have one small story from Brussels, the bombardment of 1695, and how the city came back from it.

Anna: Every speaker gets a different voice on your phone. If they sound too alike, open Settings and pick another voice for each of us.

Ben: Let's begin.

## Chapter 1: The Bombardment of 1695

Anna: So what actually happened to Brussels in August of 1695?

Ben: It was during the Nine Years' War. A French army under Marshal Villeroi marched on the city, and the aim was not to capture it. It was to punish it.

Anna: And why Brussels?

Ben: The French wanted to draw their enemies away from the siege of the fortress of Namur. Brussels was rich and poorly defended, so it made a tempting target.

Anna: That is a terrible reason to lose a city centre.

Ben: It is. About a third of the buildings in Brussels were destroyed or badly damaged, and the Grand Place was in ruins.

## Chapter 2: Rebuilding

Anna: But that is not where the story ends, is it?

Ben: No. The guilds, whose houses lined the square, rebuilt them, and the square was restored in about four years.

Anna: Which is why, when you stand in the Grand Place today, you are looking at a city that chose to rebuild rather than give up.

Ben: Well said. That's the end of the demo. If you like this format, ask for any history as a conversation between two hosts.

Anna: Thanks for listening.
