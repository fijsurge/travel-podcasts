              Phone voice

              Only used when you choose it above. It stops when you leave the app, and its voices can differ from part to part. Better voices are usually under your phone's text-to-speech settings (for example "Google" or "Enhanced" voices) and appear here once installed.

              One per line, word = how it should be said. Only affects the phone voice. To fix how the natural voices say a name, ask Claude: that change is made once in the repository and the audio is remade for every device.

              Request relay (optional)

              Lets the Create tab send requests straight to Claude. Ask Patrick for the address and the family code, or follow relay/README.md in the repository. Both stay on this phone only.

              GitHub key (optional)

              Lets this phone delete episodes from GitHub. The key stays on this phone only. On github.com open Settings, Developer settings, Fine-grained tokens. Choose Only select repositories and pick travel-podcasts. Under Repository permissions set Contents to Read and write. Pick a short expiry.
