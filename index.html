Travel Podcasts

Episodes are read aloud by your phone's own voices. Pick one and press play.

    Chapters

      Settings

      Better voices are usually under your phone's text-to-speech settings (for example "Google" or "Enhanced" voices). They appear here once installed.

      One per line, word = how it should be said. Only changes the spoken audio, never the text on screen.

      Add an episode

      Paste an episode in markdown (title line, then ## Welcome and ## Chapter … sections), or choose a .md file. Episodes added here stay on this phone. To keep one on every device, ask Claude to add it to the repository.
